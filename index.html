About

Biography

Read full biography →

Phil Zuckerman is an award-winning writer, speaker, and professor of sociology and secular studies at Pitzer College in Claremont, California.

A sociologist and author, he has appeared on the Chelsea Handler Show, Bill O’Reilly, Dennis Prager, Freethought Today, and All Things Considered. He has spoken at the Oxford Union, Chautauqua Institution, Georgetown, Brown, Caltech, UC Berkeley, Washington University, the University of Aberdeen, USC, Reed College, and the University of Copenhagen.

His scholarship has appeared in Secularism and Nonreligion, Secular Studies, Sociology of Religion, Journal of Contemporary Religion, Journal for the Scientific Study of Religion, Sociology Compass, and the Nordic Journal of Religion and Society. His essays and commentary have appeared in the Los Angeles Times, Salon, The Nation, The Conversation, The Washington Post, Free Inquiry, The Humanist, and Bloomberg.

In 2011, he founded the first Secular Studies program in the nation—an interdisciplinary field examining nonreligious people, thought, culture, political secularism, philosophical skepticism, and personal and social secularity.
