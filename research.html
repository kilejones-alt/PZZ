Research themes

An interactive field of questions connecting secular life, morality, democracy, nonreligion, social change, and humanism.

Active field

Secularism

How societies reorganize belief, institutions, and civic life as religion changes.

Institutional work

Secular Studies

In 2011, Phil Zuckerman founded the first Secular Studies program in the nation—an interdisciplinary field examining nonreligious people, thought, culture, political secularism, philosophical skepticism, and personal and social secularity.

Published scholarship

His scholarship has appeared in Secularism and Nonreligion, Secular Studies, Sociology of Religion, Journal of Contemporary Religion, Journal for the Scientific Study of Religion, Sociology Compass, and the Nordic Journal of Religion and Society.
